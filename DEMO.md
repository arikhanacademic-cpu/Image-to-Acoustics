# Recording the LinkedIn demo

Aim for 45–60 seconds. Screen at 1600×960 (or browser zoom 90 % on a laptop),
sound captured from the system. Prepare four images beforehand: a cathedral or
church, a small furnished bedroom, a tiled bathroom, and a forest or game scene.

1. **Hook, 0–5 s.** Start on the empty app. Caption: "One voice. Any room in
   a picture."
2. **Voice, 5–12 s.** Pick a voice, play its preview, type the line, press
   Generate speech. Expressive lines work best, for example: "Hello? Is
   anyone there? I can hear my own voice coming back to me."
3. **First room, 12–25 s.** Drop the cathedral image. Let the scan run, then
   press Space. Play the dry original first (A), then switch to B mid-sentence.
   The instant switch at the same moment of speech is the "wow".
4. **The physics is real, 25–38 s.** Drag the listener right up to the voice:
   it becomes close and clear. Drag it to the back of the hall: distant and
   washy. Flip "Voice faces" to Away. Each change re-renders in a few seconds.
5. **Range, 38–55 s.** Replace the image three times: bedroom (dry, intimate),
   bathroom (bright, ringing), forest (open, almost no tail). Same voice, no
   regeneration. Note that "From cache, no characters used" stays visible.
6. **Close, 55–60 s.** Hover the reverb-time chart. Caption: "Estimated from the
   image, measured on the result. Built on ElevenLabs."

Tips: wear headphones while recording so A/B switches are audible in your
reactions. Don't normalise the screen recording's audio, because the app
already level-matches A and B.
